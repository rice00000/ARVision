# Design brief

From the team's Vision Project doc and the team sketch. The doc is authoritative; this is the part the code follows.

- **Who:** children who gained sight late (Project Prakash, India, ages 8–18), very low acuity at first (about 20/235–20/400). Seated, turning, reaching.
- **Goal:** everyday vision skills, checked by no-help check-ins on new objects, not game scores. A training tool that logs progress, not mainly a research instrument.
- **Domains:** daily objects, getting around, school and reading, people. **Layers:** visual function, perception, action.
- **Rules:** audio first (children may not read yet); feedback paired across sight, sound and touch, never colour alone; help fades; help, training and check-ins stay separate; objects vary so children learn to see, not memorise; low floor, high ceiling; difficulty adapts round by round (facilitator first, staircase later).
- **AR additions (team sketch):** passthrough filters (contrast, black-and-white / greyscale), text-to-speech, RDK motion in 3D, recognising real objects (a cup) with CV, and "selectively reduce information in passthrough".
- **Hardware:** Quest 3 (no eye tracking).

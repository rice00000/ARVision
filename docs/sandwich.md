# Make a Sandwich

In **Find the object**, beside Ready to Study. Copy the sample from bottom to top: plate starts with toast, three fillings appear in a shuffled order, and toast closes the stack. The tray is shuffled separately. Pick up with either hand and let go over the plate; pieces stay flat. The sample never changes to reveal a pick.

- Correct layers snap into place. Wrong layers return with a sound and cross; off-plate drops, pauses and tracking loss are unscored. Help explains where to look.
- The finished stack rises, separates into layers, spins and settles back before the reward. Recipe, tray order, per-layer picks and assembly time are logged.

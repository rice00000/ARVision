# Home and rewards

- Point and pinch **Menu**: **Practice**, **Games**, **My room**. One bar above the room: Menu, Practice, **Shop**, **My things**.
- **Shop:** Furniture, Pets and Rooms stay along the top (a gold frame marks the open one); one pinch switches. Browse one rotating model at a time with the solid triangles; the arrow goes back to the room. The price is a stone with its number and a bar of fixed length that fills as the child's stones near it; a check mark means enough. Speech says the price and "You need N more" or "You can buy it". Buy shows only when affordable and needs a separate check-mark press. New furniture and pets go straight into the room (furniture into the box when the room is full). Sunny room is free; two more rooms cost 4/6 stones.
- **My room:** furniture can be moved at once: pinch, move/twist, release. A check/cross shows a valid/blocked spot. The box (**My things**) stores a held piece (aim with the other hand and pinch) and, with an empty hand, opens the stored furniture → Place. Each room remembers its layout.
- **Pets** (cat, dog 8 stones; panda, monkey 12; one of each). A pet earns one magic stone per 10 minutes of training (menu and room time never count) and holds one at a time. In the room it carries the stone onto the stool beside the child; point or reach and pinch the stone to take it. Hold a palm up for half a second to call the pets. A fingertip on a pet pets it: 1, then 3, 5, 7, 9 pets, each at least a minute after the last, raise its level; each level is one minute less (5 at best). Pets wander and rest on a free cushion. No pet sounds yet.
- The menu pauses training and holds pending speech. Leaving the room restores the training view. **Practice** resumes the same round.
- Adventure rewards come from each level's **First Reward** and **Repeat Reward**. Skip, timeout, abandonment, missing modules and probes earn none.

Modules: `Inventory` owns transactions; `Rooms` owns the selected room; `Decor` owns layouts; `Pets` owns the pets ([Pet](../modules/pets/pet.gd): the first behaviour that wants to act wins, see `_choose`); `Home` owns UI. Contracts live in `Events`; room rendering belongs to Reality. Each module can be removed. Item `.tres` files define models/prices. Logs include purchases, declined purchases, rewards, room changes and furniture moves.

Sources: furniture visuals/inventory/layout adapted from local XRVision. Room meshes: [Kenney Furniture Kit](https://kenney.nl/assets/furniture-kit), CC0, original license in `content/rooms/kenney/License.txt`; downloaded from [Kenney's OpenGameArt release](https://opengameart.org/content/furniture-kit). Three room layouts assembled here. Pets: [Kenney Cube Pets 2.0](https://kenney.nl/assets/cube-pets), CC0, license in `content/pets/kenney/License.txt`, with its own idle/walk clips; lying down is posed in code. Wall animation adapts the delay/gravity/rotation approach of [miwls' simple glass broken](https://godotshaders.com/shader/simple-glass-broken/), CC0, to a single mesh of actual 3D Voronoi pieces; the pieces' jagged outline is the hole. Sweep wind adapts [dip's Dynamic Wind Trail With Loops](https://godotshaders.com/shader/dynamic-wind-trail-with-loops/), CC0. Shatter (split into left/right mono, played from the hole's edges) and dot shimmer: the team's own sounds in `trials/motion/sounds/`. Onion touch uses [Godot's elastic Tween](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/classes/class_tween.html) (engine MIT); its sound is synthesized locally. No downloaded character animation or rig retargeting.

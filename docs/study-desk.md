# Ready to Study

Replaces Outline Match in Find the object, retaining level ID `outline` and its rewards. One desk, five props: mug, notebook, pencil, headphones, plant. Take from the side tray and release over a matching tabletop outline.

- All five outlines are visible initially, with identical ink. Pickup, hover and help never reveal a pairing. Help only explains comparing shapes.
- Release: correct snaps upright and hides that outline; wrong returns to the tray with feedback. Off-desk drops, pauses and tracking loss are unscored; a 0.12 s pinch dropout is tolerated.
- Five correct placements trigger a pausable walk, sit, notebook-opening and writing sequence before completion. This level uses everyday completion wording.
- Per-drop picks and `put` events, `tidy_seconds` and ending phases are logged. Fixed five-object task; no adaptive difficulty claim.

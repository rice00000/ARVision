Changes: five props centred, rescaled and rotated into desk poses; meshes grouped, roughness adjusted. Outline PNGs are orthographic projections of those meshes (`tools/prepare_desk_assets.py`). Student and chair rescaled at runtime; character and notebook cover animated by this project. Wood texture: Rob Tuytel, [Poly Haven Plywood](https://polyhaven.com/a/plywood), CC0.
